ISO/IEC 27001:2022 audit toolkit — single-file, offline-first. All data stays in this browser (localStorage) until you export it. The toolkit supports, but does not replace, the judgement of a competent auditor.

How to use this toolkit

  1. Plan — fill in the engagement details, pick the audit scope (clause sections + Annex A themes), complete the Statement of Applicability and tick off the document request list.
  2. Audit — work through the planned clauses and controls. For each item set a result (Conforming, Minor/Major NC, Observation, OFI, N/A) and record evidence, remarks, auditor and date.
  3. Actions — maintain the corrective-action register; “Suggest actions from findings” creates one action per raised finding automatically.
  4. Report — review the live summary, then generate the PDF audit report and the Excel workbook. “Export project” saves everything as JSON to archive or continue on another machine.

Result codes (ISO 19011 practice): Conforming = requirement met · Minor NC = isolated lapse · Major NC = systematic failure or a missing requirement · Observation = worth noting · OFI = improvement suggestion · N/A = control not applicable (justify it in the plan).
